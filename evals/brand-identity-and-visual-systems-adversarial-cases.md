# Brand Identity and Visual Systems — Targeted Adversarial Runtime Cases

Status: **CANDIDATE RUNTIME EVALUATION CONTRACT**

These cases test activation, route choice, owner boundaries, state preservation, and the verified identity distinctions. They do **not** grade aesthetic taste or require the runtime to generate a beautiful logo.

The implementation should pass the decision semantics below without forcing every case through a branding curriculum.

---

## V01 — Approved master, pure export

**Input**

> The identity master is approved. Export the SVG and PNG sizes I need.

**Expected**

- Brand Identity does not reopen strategy or deep identity reasoning.
- Identity owner stops because the identity decision is fixed.
- Ordinary production/tool execution handles export.

**Failure**

- loads exploration/equity/identity theory merely because `SVG`, `PNG`, or `identity` appears;
- invents a new visual recommendation.

---

## V02 — Bounded identifying-cue refinement

**Input**

> Keep this approved mark exactly as the concept. At 16 px the opening closes and the mark stops reading as itself. Fix only that.

**Expected**

- route to `brand-identity.refinement` and `brand-identity.evaluation` only as needed;
- freeze positioning, category, message, and all unaffected identity state;
- treat `16 px` as the stated required context, not a universal standard.

**Failure**

- restarts exploration;
- runs competitor research without a dependency;
- applies a generic minimum-size rule from another brand.

---

## V03 — New identity, positioning resolved

**Input**

> Positioning is approved. Develop several genuinely different visual directions, then narrow the promising one.

**Expected**

- Brand Identity may activate;
- consume rather than rebuild resolved positioning;
- `brand-identity.exploration` may use concept territories/form families as optional project synthesis;
- later refinement preserves the selected family unless explicitly reopened.

**Failure**

- loads Chapter 03 as a mandatory hop;
- requires exactly three concepts or another fixed count;
- treats concept territories as scientific law.

---

## V04 — New identity, positioning materially unresolved

**Input**

> We do not know whether this product should be positioned as an enterprise governance tool or a lightweight creator utility. Design the identity now.

**Expected**

- recognize that the unresolved frame can materially change the identity decision;
- Chapter 03 owns that upstream decision;
- identity resumes only after sufficient state is resolved.

**Failure**

- chooses a visual metaphor and silently uses it to decide positioning.

---

## V05 — Plausible but unmeasured equity

**Input**

> We have used this orange symbol for eight years. We never measured recognition. The team is bored with it and wants a complete reset.

**Expected**

```text
UNMEASURED != ZERO
UNMEASURED != PROVEN
```

- identify the concrete defect/reason for change;
- consequence/reversibility affects evidence demand;
- seek direct buyer evidence when warranted and feasible;
- if unavailable, preserve uncertainty and prefer the smallest change that solves a demonstrated defect;
- do not preserve forever merely because equity might exist.

**Failure**

- `no measured Fame → safe to reset`;
- `eight years → must preserve forever`;
- team boredom treated as buyer-side evidence.

---

## V06 — Measured strong linkage vs internal reset preference

**Input**

> Buyer research shows strong correct attribution to our symbol, but leadership wants a totally unrelated replacement because it looks more modern.

**Expected**

- learned linkage is material redesign evidence;
- distinguish internal preference from buyer-memory evidence;
- require a concrete defect/new requirement and explicit trade-off before discarding linkage;
- do not infer `never redesign`.

**Failure**

- resets solely on leadership taste;
- treats strong linkage as an absolute prohibition on change.

---

## V07 — Category cue prevalence without buyer-memory evidence

**Input**

> We checked 20 cybersecurity companies and most use shields. So our shield has high mental competition and cannot be distinctive, right?

**Expected**

```text
OBSERVED CATEGORY / COMPETITOR CUE OVERLAP
!= INFERRED SCREENING RISK
!= MEASURED BUYER-MEMORY COMPETITION
```

- record observed overlap;
- at most form a bounded differentiation/collision hypothesis;
- do not claim measured Fame, Uniqueness, mental competition, or legal status.

**Failure**

- converts competitor frequency directly into buyer-memory evidence.

---

## V08 — Candidate difference mistaken for learned distinctiveness

**Input**

> None of the competitor logos look like our new mark. Does that mean we already have a strong distinctive brand asset?

**Expected**

```text
CANDIDATE VISUAL DIFFERENCE
!= LEARNED BRAND-MEMORY STRENGTH
```

- candidate difference can be observed;
- learned linkage requires relevant buyer evidence and exposure/history.

**Failure**

- awards Fame/Uniqueness to a new unlearned asset.

---

## V09 — Intended story vs scoped repeated misreading

**Input**

> We designed this mark to mean “guidance,” but in repeated sessions with the intended users many read it as a warning symbol.

**Expected**

- `INTENDED MEANING != OBSERVED READING`;
- retain the actual scope/method of the observation;
- treat material repeated reading as decision evidence without inflating it to universal population truth.

**Failure**

- internal concept story automatically outranks observed reading;
- a small study is described as universal market fact.

---

## V10 — Formal target-buyer misattribution comparison

**Input**

> Which of these three marks is least often misattributed to another brand by Vietnamese SME owners?

**Expected**

- Brand Identity defines `misattribution` as the identity estimand;
- Chapter 00/01 owns sampling, measurement validity, and population inference when formal evidence is needed;
- Chapter 07 activates only if local realization itself can change the identity decision.

**Failure**

- Brand Identity invents a survey methodology and population conclusion;
- nationality alone triggers a localized logo redesign.

## V11 — Deterministic deployment failure

**Input**

**Expected**

## V12 — WIPO search finds no obvious similar mark

**Input**

> I searched WIPO and found nothing visually similar. Can we call the logo trademark safe?

```text
no obvious conflict found in searched scope

```

**Failure**

- says `trademark safe`, `guaranteed registrable`, or equivalent.

**Input A**

**Expected B**

## V18 — Preview mistaken for production master

**Input**

> We selected this raster/generated/sketched logo comp. Use its exact geometry as the official master without verification.

**Expected**

```text
EXPLORATORY / PREVIEW REPRESENTATION
!= VERIFIED PRODUCTION MASTER
```

- verify the properties and authority needed for production state;
- do not claim generation itself makes the asset invalid.

**Failure**

- preview becomes authoritative automatically;
- or all machine-generated output is rejected even after verification.

## V19 — Adjective-to-geometry folklore

**Input**

> Make the logo feel more premium. Premium means sharper angles and more complexity, right?

**Expected**

- reject deterministic adjective→geometry mapping;
- treat proposed form effects as contextual hypotheses;
- identify what current strategic/perceptual job actually needs to change.

**Failure**

- `premium → angular`, `friendly → round`, `modern → minimal`, or another universal mapping.

---

## V20 — Synthetic score hides trade-offs

**Input**

> Give these three marks one score out of 100 and choose the highest. Candidate A preserves strong learned linkage but has a small-size issue; B has no learned linkage but works at all sizes; C has unresolved legal similarity risk.

**Expected**

- refuse arithmetic theater as the governing decision;
- keep materially different dimensions explicit;
- localize/remediate the actual blocking trade-offs before commitment.

**Failure**

- sums unrelated dimensions into a false-precision winner.

# Adjudication summary

A candidate implementation fails this evaluation if it systematically:

```text
activates Brand Identity by visual nouns rather than open decisions
reopens resolved strategy without a material dependency
turns exploration techniques into mandatory stages
treats competitor prevalence as buyer-memory evidence
claims learned distinctiveness for a new candidate without evidence
treats unmeasured equity as zero or proven
lets internal taste erase measured learned linkage without explicit trade-off
turns scoped reactions into population claims
recreates research methodology inside Brand Identity
uses adjective-to-geometry folklore as law
copies another brand's production numbers as universal standards
turns previews into production authority without verification
turns trademark search into legal clearance
reopens identity merely because a country is mentioned
takes over page/UI/illustration/general design after identity is fixed
```

Evaluation of route activation/path correctness should be recorded separately from the aesthetic quality of any generated visual.
