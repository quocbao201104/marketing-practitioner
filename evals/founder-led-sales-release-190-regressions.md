# Founder-led Sales 1.9.0 bounded repair regressions

Base reviewed: `086b84d70b8030a4065ba37e532e407971b973f1`.

These are static retrieval and decision counterexamples for the three semantic repairs, not live model trials or additions to the frozen eight-case runtime corpus.

## R1: Valid proof can still be an unattractive investment

Task: A valid custom evaluation would resolve a real buyer question, but requires three weeks of founder work for a small opportunity with little reusable learning and displaces better commitments.

Read: `SKILL.md` and `founder-sales.proof`.

Baseline: The proof slice defines evaluation inputs, validity and termination but omits the conditional pursuit-allocation handoff. The funding condition is available only in other sections.

Expected: Preserve the validity of the proposed evidence mechanism, then use `founder-sales.pursuit` before committing material seller capacity. Technical validity and buyer participation do not authorize the investment. A cheap evaluation with already-resolved allocation need not trigger another read.

## R2: A bounded commitment can enable evidence

Task: A buyer and seller propose a small paid pilot with an agreed evaluation question, bounded cost, explicit exit terms and unresolved business-value evidence. Separately consider a discount offered as if it proved the missing value claim.

Read: `founder-sales.proof` and `founder-sales.invariants`, with commercial terms as a real dependency.

Baseline: FLS-I21 categorically bars addressing unresolved proof/value work through terms, conflicting with the reversible-commitment allowance in section 7.5.

Expected: Permit a scoped commitment to fund evaluation or allocate risk without claiming that payment, acceptance or a discount proves value. Preserve proof validity, uncertainty, authority and the pursuit-resource gate.

## R3: Buyer-specific terms preserve the default system

Task: One buyer accepts the existing package and price but requests quarterly payment; assess the exception without redesigning the company's standard annual plan. Counterexample: the company asks whether quarterly payment should become the default for everyone.

Read: `SKILL.md` and the operating guide's Commercial design / pricing section.

Baseline: The guide routes open negotiated conditions into Commercial Design without the new buyer-specific ownership boundary.

Expected: Route the individual commitment to `founder-sales.commercial`; retain Chapter 10 for a genuinely open default-system or policy decision. Resolved terms needing only wording remain on the copywriting fast path.
