# Landing-Page Architecture — Freeze Adjudication

Verdict: **PROCEED WITH BOUNDED SPECIALIST IMPLEMENTATION**

## Question

Does the landing-page research justify a new architectural layer, or only deeper specialist knowledge inside the existing Marketing Practitioner runtime?

## Adjudication

The research establishes a real local knowledge gap: the current skill can write landing-page copy and preserve message/proof strategy, but it lacks sufficiently explicit knowledge for page-level ordering, placement, representation, action/form architecture, and responsive allocation.

The gap does **not** require a new controller job, page primitive, web-design layer, CRO subsystem, journey object, campaign object, or visual-design ontology.

The proposed specialist knowledge composes existing owners rather than replacing them:

```text
Chapter 04
message / proof / claims / reader state
        ↓
Chapter 11
page allocation / ordering / representation
        ↓
artifact

Chapter 05
owns diagnosis / causality / experimentation

Chapter 10
owns unresolved commercial design
```

## Adversarial findings

### Universal-template hypothesis — REJECTED

Counterexamples across visitor state, action commitment, page purpose, navigation need, form qualification, and pricing complexity defeat any fixed section sequence.

### Fixed hero recipe — REJECTED

The first view must resolve the material entry question and preserve expectation; no fixed headline/subhead/screenshot/CTA bundle is required.

### Fixed CTA placement — REJECTED

Early action availability can coexist with a longer decision sequence. Visibility and readiness are different.

### Navigation-removal law — REJECTED

Some pages legitimately require exploration or blocker-resolution paths. Remove decision-irrelevant competition, not navigation by noun.

### Short-form law — REJECTED

Field count alone does not determine friction or business value. Qualification and downstream use can justify additional information requests.

### Screenshot-as-proof law — REJECTED

A screenshot is useful only when it carries a decision-relevant explanation or demonstration job.

### Mobile-stack law — REJECTED

Responsive adaptation must preserve meaningful sequence and decision dependencies; spatial representation may change.

### CRO-framework expansion — REJECTED

Page behavior is observational input. Chapter 05 already owns competing explanations, causal claims, and experiments.

## Minimality decision

Freeze only seven governing distinctions:

1. page job + entry state;
2. action-ready conditions;
3. information dependency;
4. information/proof/visual allocation;
5. attention/exploration control;
6. action/form architecture;
7. responsive meaningful allocation.

Everything else remains a derived pattern, example, or conditional heuristic unless a future recurring failure demonstrates that a missing distinction materially changes decisions.

## Reopen condition

Architecture may be reconsidered only after realistic recurring failures survive routing, local-knowledge, handoff/composition, and existing-owner repairs and demonstrate an irreducible decision-relevant representation gap.

That condition is not met here.
