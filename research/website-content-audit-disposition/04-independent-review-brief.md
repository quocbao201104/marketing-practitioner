## 9. Cross-subject relationship attack

This is the strongest claimed missing capability.

Attempt cases where:

- same topic serves different functions;
- different wording is functionally redundant;
- one subject serves several material functions;
- one function is distributed across several subjects;
- relations change after a repair;
- consolidation lacks a viable survivor;
- a survivor preserves wording but loses authority/currentness;
- a survivor breaks an operational/discovery dependency;
- a gap appears only because audit scope omitted an existing representation.

Determine whether these cases require dedicated audit knowledge or whether existing object / representation / audience-state / dependency / evidence grammar already handles them losslessly.

Do not invent a global relation taxonomy unless a concrete failure requires one.

## 10. Disposition attack
