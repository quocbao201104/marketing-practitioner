## BI-T01 — Evidence-status leakage

### Reviewer finding

`concept territories → form families → controlled mutation → system commit` could be read as theory with the same epistemic status as empirical distinctiveness/equity findings.

### Repair

Major theory statements are explicitly tagged or grouped by evidence status:

```text
EMPIRICAL / ACADEMIC
PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE
PROJECT SYNTHESIS
CONTEXTUAL HYPOTHESIS
```

`EXPLORATION != REFINEMENT` remains a state-preservation distinction.

Controlled one-variable mutation is downgraded to an **optional project/practitioner synthesis** useful when it makes the reason for change inspectable, especially under generative or iterative drift. It is not required when an alternative deterministic or human design process preserves the same state and decision quality.

## BI-T02 — Mental competition / ownability wording exceeded evidence

### Reviewer finding

Observed visual prevalence among competitors is not buyer-memory evidence and must not be converted directly into `mental competition`, `low ownability`, Fame, or Uniqueness claims.

### Repair

The post-repair theory freezes:

```text
OBSERVED CATEGORY / COMPETITOR CUE OVERLAP
!=
INFERRED CONFUSION OR SCREENING RISK
!=
MEASURED BUYER-MEMORY COMPETITION
```

`Fame`, `Uniqueness`, and measured buyer-memory competition require suitable market evidence.

`Ownability potential` is removed from governing language. Where a screening concept is useful, the theory uses **candidate differentiation / collision hypothesis** and labels it a contextual design-screening hypothesis, not legal or market-memory evidence.

## BI-T03 — Stop boundary with pure design execution

### Reviewer finding

Geometry, typography, imagery, iconography, patterns, and motion could allow the owner to absorb generic production work after the identity decision is already fixed.

### Repair

A hard stop test is added:

```text
BRAND-IDENTIFYING CUE / RELATIONSHIP / IDENTITY DECISION IS OPEN
→ visual-identity owner may remain active

IDENTITY DECISION IS FIXED
+ only production manipulation / application execution remains
→ ordinary design/tool execution or the relevant downstream owner
```

An aperture-at-16-px problem may still use the refinement fast path when the defect can change the usability of the identifying asset, but pure Bezier cleanup, export, resizing, layout execution, campaign illustration, or UI production is not promoted into governed marketing knowledge merely because a brand asset is present.

---

## BI-T04 — Perceptual testing handoff

### Reviewer finding

The identity layer should define the identity decision and what must be learned, but should not silently recreate sampling, measurement, inference, or causal methodology owned elsewhere.

### Repair

The post-repair theory separates:

```text
IDENTITY OWNER
→ defines the decision-relevant perceptual estimand / failure condition

EXISTING RESEARCH / EXPERIMENT OWNERS
→ own sample design, measurement validity, population inference,
  and causality when formal evidence is required
```

Direct deterministic observations such as a counter closing at a required pixel size do not require a research or experiment handoff.

---

## BI-T05 — Unmeasured equity lacked action discipline

### Reviewer finding

`plausible but unmeasured equity` could justify either indefinite preservation or unjustified reset.

### Repair

The post-repair theory freezes:

```text
UNMEASURED != ZERO
UNMEASURED != PROVEN
```

For higher-consequence or difficult-to-reverse replacement, seek stronger direct buyer evidence when feasible.

When suitable evidence is unavailable:

```text
preserve uncertainty
→ prefer the smallest change that solves the demonstrated defect
→ avoid claiming that asset strength was proven
→ define what evidence would justify a larger future change
```

This is a proportional decision rule, not a universal command to preserve legacy assets.

## NB-01 — Preview/master wording generalization

The independent reviewer noted that `GENERATED CONCEPT IMAGE != PRODUCTION MASTER` is directionally correct but coupled to current tool limitations.

The durable form becomes:

```text
EXPLORATORY / PREVIEW REPRESENTATION
!=
VERIFIED PRODUCTION MASTER
```

AI generation remains one important case, but the principle applies equally to raster comps, sketches, mockups, traced vectors, or other unverified previews.
