## 1. Research question

> Given sufficiently resolved brand/market strategy and a defined identity job, what is the smallest governed decision model an AI marketing practitioner needs to create, revise, evaluate, preserve, and steward brand-identifying visual assets while preserving upstream state, distinguishing candidate observations from learned equity and legal status, testing real perceptual/deployment failure, and stopping before generic design execution?

The target remains a bounded marketing decision owner, not a graphic-design curriculum.

---

## 2. Gap that survives review

The independent review found that existing owners can already handle many adjacent concerns:

```text
Chapter 03 → positioning / value / strategic distinctiveness inputs
Chapter 07 → materially local realization decisions
Chapter 11 → page-level visual allocation after identity state exists
research / experimentation owners → formal measurement and inference
ordinary tools / designers → pure production execution
legal specialists / official systems → clearance and legal judgment
```

What remains unowned is narrower:

```text
RESOLVED BRAND / MARKET STATE
+ OPEN BRAND-IDENTIFYING VISUAL DECISION
+ RELEVANT EXISTING-ASSET / DEPLOYMENT EVIDENCE
→ BOUNDED IDENTITY DECISION
```

The strongest surviving failure is **asset-level identity realization and stewardship**, especially when the decision concerns:

- whether learned or plausible existing equity should be preserved, evolved, or replaced;
- whether a candidate is being mistaken for a learned distinctive asset;
- whether observed public reading invalidates an internal rationale;
- whether an identifying asset survives the contexts in which it must function;
- what reusable identity state must be committed for downstream consistent use.

A pure geometry or export task does not itself justify this owner.

## 3. Owner boundary and hard stop

Candidate owner:

```text
BRAND-IDENTIFYING VISUAL ASSET REALIZATION / STEWARDSHIP
```

It may remain active only while a **brand-identifying cue, relationship, preservation decision, or identity-system decision is materially open**.

### Hard stop

```text
BRAND-IDENTIFYING CUE / RELATIONSHIP / IDENTITY DECISION IS OPEN
→ visual-identity owner may act

IDENTITY DECISION IS FIXED
+ only production manipulation / application execution remains
→ ordinary design/tool execution or the relevant downstream owner
```

Examples normally outside this owner once identity state is fixed:

```text
Bezier cleanup with no identity consequence
routine SVG export
format conversion
mechanical resizing
campaign illustration execution
page layout
UI component styling
application-specific art direction
```

A small-size geometry defect may still remain an identity refinement task when it changes whether the identifying cue remains perceivable or usable in a required identity context.

The owner does **not** own:

```text
POSITIONING / VALUE
MESSAGE / CLAIM / COPY
GENERAL LOCALIZATION
PAGE / UI / PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
GENERAL GRAPHIC DESIGN
GENERAL ART DIRECTION
FORMAL RESEARCH METHODOLOGY
CAUSAL INFERENCE
LEGAL CLEARANCE
```

## 4. Epistemic-status discipline

The runtime must not treat every useful identity practice as the same kind of knowledge.

Use these statuses when the distinction matters:

```text
EMPIRICAL / ACADEMIC
Observed relationship or bounded finding supported by research.
Scope and moderators must be preserved.

PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE
Production/stewardship convention supported by experienced practice
or authoritative brand-system guidance, without causal business claim.

PROJECT SYNTHESIS
A practitioner decision method assembled by this project because it
makes state, trade-offs, or iteration inspectable. Not academic law.

CONTEXTUAL HYPOTHESIS
A plausible candidate-specific prediction that must be checked against
current evidence rather than generalized as a rule.
```

Examples:

- `REDESIGN != RESET` and the need to distinguish learned buyer linkage from internal judgment are supported by empirical branding research.
- clear-space relationships, master/variant governance, and production files are professional practice.
- concept territories, form families, and controlled one-variable mutation are project/practitioner synthesis.
- predictions such as “this curvature may read warmer here” are contextual hypotheses unless directly established for the relevant decision.

## 5. Core invariants

### I1 — Start from the open identity decision

Artifact nouns do not activate a full branding process.

Possible open states include:

```text
create
review
preserve / evolve / replace
explore
refine
stress-test
commit / steward
```

A supplied mark with one small-size failure should remain narrow unless another dependency can materially change the decision.

### I2 — Preserve resolved upstream state

Do not reopen positioning, audience, category, message, commercial state, or localization merely because visual identity work is occurring.

Reopen an upstream owner only when a contradiction or missing decision materially prevents the current identity decision.

### I3 — Existing equity is uncertain state, not decoration

```text
REDESIGN != RESET
UNMEASURED != ZERO
UNMEASURED != PROVEN
```

Distinguish, when evidence permits:

```text
measured / strongly evidenced learned asset linkage
plausible but unmeasured existing equity
little or no meaningful evidence of existing equity
```

Historical use alone does not prove buyer memory. Lack of formal tracking does not justify assuming zero equity.

For a higher-consequence or difficult-to-reverse replacement, seek stronger direct buyer evidence when feasible.

When suitable evidence is unavailable:

```text
preserve uncertainty
→ prefer the smallest change that solves the demonstrated defect
→ do not claim asset strength was proven
→ identify what future evidence would justify a larger change
```

This is a proportional default under uncertainty, not a command to preserve legacy assets forever.

### I4 — Intended meaning and observed reading are separate

```text
INTENDED MEANING != OBSERVED PERCEPTUAL READING
```

An internal story cannot override material unintended readings or deployment failures.

But observed readings must retain their evidence scope. A handful of reactions do not automatically become a target-population claim.

### I5 — Identity owner defines the perceptual question; research owners govern formal inference

When the decision depends on formal evidence about how a population perceives, recognizes, links, or confuses an asset:

```text
IDENTITY OWNER
→ defines the decision-relevant estimand / failure condition

EXISTING RESEARCH / EXPERIMENT OWNERS
→ own sampling, measurement validity, population inference,
  experiment design, and causality when those are required
```

Examples of identity estimands may include:

```text
misattribution to another brand
correct brand linkage
unintended semantic reading
recognition under a specified exposure condition
```

Direct deterministic observations such as `counter closes at 16 px in the required master` do not need a research handoff.

### I6 — Visual properties are contextual variables, not aesthetic laws

Reject universal transformations such as:

```text
simpler = better
minimal = modern
abstract = distinctive
literal = weak
round = friendly
angular = premium
symmetry = stronger
```

Empirical work can inform bounded hypotheses about properties such as complexity, naturalness, descriptiveness, shape, symmetry, color, movement, or typography. Preserve the studied context and moderators.

### I7 — Observed category overlap is not buyer-memory competition

Freeze these states separately:

```text
OBSERVED CATEGORY / COMPETITOR CUE OVERLAP
!=
INFERRED CONFUSION OR SCREENING RISK
!=
MEASURED BUYER-MEMORY COMPETITION
```

A competitor/category audit can establish that many sampled brands use similar cues. It cannot by itself establish buyer Fame, Uniqueness, mental competition, or legal confusion.

Terms such as `Fame`, `Uniqueness`, and measured buyer-memory competition require appropriate market evidence.

A candidate may be flagged for a **differentiation/collision hypothesis** during design screening, but that is a contextual hypothesis rather than evidence of market-memory weakness or legal non-ownability.

### I8 — Candidate difference is not learned distinctive-asset strength

```text
CANDIDATE VISUAL DIFFERENCE
!= LEARNED BRAND-MEMORY STRENGTH
!= LEGAL DISTINCTIVENESS / CLEARANCE
```

A new mark may appear visually unlike sampled competitors and still have no evidence of learned buyer linkage.

Do not claim Fame or established Uniqueness for a newly created asset without suitable market evidence.

### I9 — Exploration and refinement are different state modes

```text
EXPLORATION
!= CONTROLLED REFINEMENT
```

Exploration may reopen concept or visual grammar.

Refinement should preserve whatever family/identity state has already been committed unless the user or evidence reopens exploration.

This state distinction is durable. Specific exploration devices are not mandatory.

### I10 — Controlled mutation is optional practitioner synthesis

A useful refinement method is to alter only a small number of deliberate form variables so the reason for change remains observable, for example:

```text
aperture
mass / stroke
counterspace
terminal
corner language
spacing
optical balance
```

This is **PROJECT SYNTHESIS**, not an empirical law and not a mandatory ritual.

A deterministic vector workflow, skilled human process, or other method is equally acceptable when it preserves the selected identity state and makes the consequential change sufficiently inspectable.

### I11 — Deployment tests follow the required identity context

A candidate should be checked in contexts that can actually invalidate the identity decision, such as when required:

```text
small digital display
favicon / app mark
monochrome
positive / reversed use
light / dark context
compact / horizontal relationship
physical reproduction
```

These are examples, not a universal checklist or required pixel set.

Production guidance from another brand must not be copied as universal minimum-size law.

### I12 — Identity system scope is job-relative

Possible reusable identity assets include:

```text
symbol / wordmark / lockup
color relationships
typography roles
iconographic language
reusable imagery cues
patterns / shapes
motion relationships
other persistent brand-identifying cues
```

Their presence in the possible system does not make all production involving them identity work.

The identity owner governs only unresolved **persistent/reusable brand-identifying cues and relationships**. Application-specific execution belongs downstream once those relationships are fixed.

### I13 — Preview representation is not production authority

```text
EXPLORATORY / PREVIEW REPRESENTATION
!= VERIFIED PRODUCTION MASTER
```

This applies to generated images, raster comps, sketches, mockups, traced vectors, or other previews.

Do not infer exact geometry, licensing, provenance, originality, rights ownership, master relationships, or production readiness merely from an unverified preview.

A tool may produce a true production master when its output is actually verified as such; generation itself is not disqualifying.

### I14 — Legal pre-flight is a bounded handoff

The identity owner may surface obvious conflict signals, search scope, and the need for official/specialist review when consequential.

Permitted scoped states include:

```text
no obvious conflict found in searched scope
potential conflict found
search scope incomplete
professional clearance required for consequential use
```

Never collapse visual difference, database search, or marketing distinctiveness into `trademark safe` or guaranteed registrability.

### I15 — New market does not automatically reopen identity

```text
NEW MARKET != NEW VISUAL IDENTITY
```

Chapter 07 remains the owner of localization. Reopen only the affected identity dimension when local evidence reveals a material meaning, legibility, script, symbolism, regulatory, or deployment difference.

### I16 — Do not hide trade-offs in one quality score

Do not reduce candidates to a single synthetic score that pretends unlike dimensions are directly commensurable.

Potential decision dimensions may include, when operationally relevant:

```text
strategic congruence
existing-equity preservation
observed perceptual failure
deployment fitness
observed category/competitor overlap
measured buyer-memory evidence
legal / rights uncertainty
```

Terms such as `formal coherence`, `semantic cliché density`, or `system extensibility` may be used as critique prompts only when their meaning is made concrete for the current decision. They are not presumed measurable marketing constructs.

## 6. JIT decision areas

These are addressable decision areas, not a mandatory sequence and not yet approved runtime routes.

```text
A. IDENTITY SCOPE
What brand-identifying visual decision is open?

B. MINIMUM STRATEGIC INPUT
Which already-resolved upstream facts constrain it?

C. EXISTING-EQUITY / OBSERVED CATEGORY CONTEXT
What measured or uncertain prior asset state, and what directly observed
category/competitor cue overlap, can materially change the decision?

D. EXPLORATION
Only when genuinely different identity hypotheses remain open.
Concept territories or form families may be useful project synthesis,
not required stages.

E. REFINEMENT
Only when identity state is sufficiently fixed and a form/relationship
change can still alter the brand-identifying asset decision.

F. PERCEPTUAL / DEPLOYMENT EVALUATION
Define the relevant failure condition. Use direct observation where enough;
handoff to existing research/experiment owners when formal inference is needed.

G. DISTINCTIVENESS / EQUITY ADJUDICATION
Keep candidate observations, measured buyer-memory evidence, and existing-equity
uncertainty separate.

H. LEGAL / RIGHTS HANDOFF
Only when consequential and only within bounded non-authoritative scope.

I. SYSTEM COMMIT / STEWARDSHIP
Preserve the minimum sufficient verified master assets, persistent relationships,
variants, constraints, and known uncertainty needed for future consistent use.

STOP. PURE EXECUTION
Once identity state is fixed and only mechanical or application-specific production
remains, leave the identity owner.
```

## 7. Fast-path examples

### Narrow identity refinement

```text
"The approved app mark loses its identifying opening at 16 px. Preserve the mark but repair that failure."
→ A → E → F → I
```

No positioning or category audit is reopened.

### Pure production after identity is fixed

```text
"The master is approved. Export SVG and PNG sizes."
→ STOP identity owner
→ ordinary production/tool execution
```

### New brand with resolved strategy

```text
"Positioning is approved. Develop a visual identity for the new product."
→ A → B → C only if relevant → D → E as needed → F → G → H when consequential → I
```

Concept territories/form families are optional ways to structure D, not mandatory gates.

### Rebrand with uncertain equity

```text
"This symbol has been used for eight years but we never measured recognition. Should we replace it?"
→ A → C → G
→ UNMEASURED != ZERO and != PROVEN
→ seek direct evidence when consequence warrants and feasible
→ otherwise prefer the smallest change that solves the demonstrated defect
```

### Formal perceptual comparison

```text
"Which option is less often misattributed to another brand by target buyers?"
→ identity owner defines misattribution as the relevant estimand
→ research owner governs sample/measurement/inference
→ identity owner uses the resulting scoped evidence in G
```

### Localization

```text
"Use our global identity in Japan unless something materially breaks."
→ identity state remains frozen
→ Chapter 07 evaluates local evidence
→ reopen only the affected identity dimension if evidence requires it
```

## 8. State handoffs

### Positioning → identity

Pass only established state that can change the identity decision, for example:

```text
target context
category / frame
relevant alternatives
primary value / strategic differentiation
brand architecture relationship when authoritative
material constraints
```

### Existing assets → redesign

Pass:

```text
assets currently in use
usage history
where they appear
measured buyer-memory evidence when available
plausible but unmeasured equity explicitly labeled as such
known deployment defects
material production constraints
```

### Identity → research / experimentation

Pass:

```text
decision to be informed
estimand / observable failure condition
candidate assets and fixed context
claims that must not exceed the resulting evidence
```

Do not pre-decide sample, inference, or causality merely because the identity owner requested evidence.

### Identity → downstream application

Pass:

```text
verified master assets
persistent asset relationships / variants
established color / typography roles when applicable
minimum usage constraints that protect the identity state
known unresolved risks
```

Downstream owners decide page allocation, UI behavior, campaign composition, illustration execution, and other application-specific work.

## 9. Evidence-status examples

The following are intentionally not promoted to one status:

| Statement / mechanism | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Existing learned linkage can make redesign consequential | EMPIRICAL / ACADEMIC, bounded by studied evidence |
| Marketer judgment of asset strength may diverge from consumer data | EMPIRICAL / ACADEMIC |
| Logo properties can influence perception with important moderators | EMPIRICAL / ACADEMIC |
| Preserve master/variant relationships and usage constraints | PROFESSIONAL PRACTICE |
| Concept territories for divergent exploration | PROJECT SYNTHESIS |
| Form families as an exploration decomposition | PROJECT SYNTHESIS |
| Controlled one-variable mutation | PROJECT SYNTHESIS, optional |
| “This cue may collide with category expectations” without buyer data | CONTEXTUAL HYPOTHESIS |
| Competitor sample contains many shield marks | DIRECT OBSERVATION within sampled scope |
| Buyers strongly associate shield imagery with competitor X | Requires suitable BUYER-MEMORY EVIDENCE |

Source existence does not itself authorize a universal runtime rule.

## 11. Runtime-promotion gate after repair

A bounded runtime design may be considered only if independent verification confirms that the five review findings are closed without introducing new scope.

Required conditions:

```text
1. REAL NARROW GAP
Asset-level brand-identifying realization/stewardship cannot be cleanly
substituted by existing owners.

2. EPISTEMIC STATUS
Empirical claims, professional practice, project synthesis, and contextual
hypotheses are not flattened into equal-strength rules.

3. MEMORY CLAIM DISCIPLINE
Observed category overlap does not become buyer-memory competition.

4. HARD STOP
Fixed identity + pure production leaves the identity owner.

5. RESEARCH HANDOFF
Identity defines what must be learned; existing research/experiment owners
govern formal inference when required.

6. EQUITY ACTION DISCIPLINE
Unmeasured equity is neither zero nor proven; irreversible change receives
proportionate evidence discipline.

7. JIT COMPOSITION
Narrow tasks do not load a branding curriculum.

8. LEGAL / LOCALIZATION BOUNDARIES
Legal expertise and Chapter 07 retain their existing ownership.
```
