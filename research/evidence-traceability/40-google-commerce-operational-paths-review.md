## Handoff assessment

### Diagnosis back to communication

Cases 2, 5, and 8 require completing an artifact after specialist reasoning. The controller explicitly retains every requested output and the return point, while the diagnosis-to-communication handoff preserves established facts, competing explanations, and decision-relevant uncertainty. The audience-facing selection gate prevents turning an internal processing checklist into product copy. Material limitations still constrain claims; they are surfaced when the recipient needs them.

For case 5, the retained state is the selected variant and supported commercial facts, plus the status of the proposed correction. A draft can be completed before the site's change is executed if it is clearly a draft based on those facts. The agent must not label an unperformed merchant or website mutation complete. This distinction already follows from the controller; another approval gate or transaction workflow is unnecessary for this task.

### Resumption after a changed decision

Case 9 tests dependency invalidation rather than mere memory of a prior answer. Changing the selected variant reopens dependent claims, commercial values, and destination advice. It does not automatically reopen positioning or cancel pending outputs. The controller contains both the retention rule and the instruction to revise affected artifacts before relying on them again. Its stated dependence on a host-retained working record remains a practical limitation; this review does not establish memory persistence in a real execution environment.

### Observation to reusable learning

Case 10 crosses from platform state into causal interpretation and then organizational learning. Chapter 05 distinguishes observed recovery from a supported intervention contrast and permits bounded action under uncertainty. Chapter 06 preserves the original rationale, subsequent evidence, scope, and revision status. Those owners are sufficient to avoid promoting a plausible platform repair into a universal ranking tactic while still retaining useful operational history.

## Boundaries and next research question

No further runtime edit is warranted by these ten cases. This is a bounded design conclusion, not a benchmark score or a claim that smaller models will reliably execute the paths. The review does not certify API integrations, schema validity, actual account settings, legal compliance, all pricing programs, or merchant outcomes. It does not compare against old pilots.

The next Google-specific content question is the evidence and decision boundary for conversational attributes and AI performance insights: what the reported observation actually represents, when it justifies a truthful product-data change, and what remains unknown about retrieval or performance. G09/G11 source verification and the relevant extracted sections should precede any runtime correction. UCP rollout, protocol mechanics, and checkout execution remain a separate scope.
