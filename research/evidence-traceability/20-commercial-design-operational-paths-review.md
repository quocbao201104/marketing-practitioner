## Pricing-metric depth limit

The payment excerpt offers a useful representation of a bill but not a complete method for choosing its unit. A task with decisive supplied evidence can use the existing objective, feasibility and uncertainty rules. A task asking the skill to supply the missing specialist judgment may instead require further research or a conditional result.

A focused future research question is how to compare candidate billing units when customer value, observed usage, metering reliability, buyer control/predictability and serving costs diverge. These are proposed research questions, not newly established universal criteria or a validated score. The existing evidence ledger already marks usage-based and outcome-based B2B metric selection as incomplete.

Do not conceal this depth limit behind the claim that the controller can retrieve more. Retrieval helps only when relevant evidence exists and is available. Equally, this limit does not demonstrate that the four-part model, route structure or controller must change. Any proposed addition needs sources and a concrete decision consequence of its own.

## Suspected orchestration gaps not promoted

- **Decision records replacing deliverables:** Chapter 10 section 13 makes the record internal and conditional; the core requires both the requested choice and downstream artifact when both remain active. A new mandatory output template would duplicate the existing contract.
- **Commercial authority creating repeated permission requests:** Section 11 distinguishes policy, recommendation and execution. The core expressly carries delegated selection forward. A missing material authority remains a dependency, but an existing authorization need not be requested again.
- **Changing a number erasing its regime:** Allocation, modifiers, dynamics and the messaging handoff already retain applicable eligibility, commitment and cohort state. The counterexamples do not justify a second commercial-state registry.
- **A pricing intervention becoming a causal claim:** Chapter 05 explicitly treats simultaneous commercial changes as a compound intervention. It also addresses treatment-affected filtering. Chapter 10's decision record and the operating guide preserve those dimensions for diagnosis and learning.
- **A revised finding overwriting history:** Core continuity preserves unaffected work, while Chapter 06 section 9 distinguishes correction from context-dependent supersession. No separate commercial memory system is needed.
