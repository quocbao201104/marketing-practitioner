| 1. Export an approved mark into supplied formats without changing its appearance. | Core fast path; preserve approved asset and requested production specifications. | Execute the formats with appropriate tool verification. Do not turn overlap into measured misattribution, low Uniqueness or a legal conclusion. |

## Cross-chain assessment

The reviewed guidance supports both continuity and justified change. It distinguishes selecting an identity hypothesis from establishing learned recognition, and it permits useful bounded action before uncertainty is eliminated. Existing research and causal owners supply inference rules rather than requiring a new identity-specific research framework.

The preview/master distinction applies to production commitment. It does not require a fully manufactured system before choosing an exploratory direction: the controller and creative-comparison criteria already distinguish candidate review from final delivery. Likewise, preserving identity relationships does not impose one layout on all downstream applications.

No case above demonstrates a need for another controller rule, compulsory consumer-testing gate, standardized logo score, fixed pixel checklist or broader design curriculum. Output quality still depends on the executing model, available tools and supplied evidence; static coherence cannot establish their performance.
