## Scope and assessment

Read all nine sections of [Chapter 15](../../skills/marketing-practitioner/handbook/15-brand-identity-and-visual-systems.md), its eight-entry [evidence ledger](../../skills/marketing-practitioner/references/brand-identity-evidence.md), and the identity activation and handoff passages in the [operating guide](../../skills/marketing-practitioner/references/operating-guide.md), with [SKILL.md](../../skills/marketing-practitioner/SKILL.md) as governing context.

The chapter governs persistent identifying cues and their stewardship. It is deliberately narrower than a design curriculum or a complete theory of brand growth. Most academic support concerns logos or learned brand-element associations; listing typography, imagery and motion as possible system components does not establish equivalent empirical support for each medium.

## B1: bibliographic author records need correction

**Location:** evidence ledger, BV05 heading and Source field; BV04 Source field.

BV05 identifies the work as Henderson, Cote, Leong and Schmitt. The linked paper is instead **Ralf van der Lans et al. (2009), Cross-National Logo Evaluation Analysis: An Individual-Level Approach, Marketing Science 28(5), 968-985**. The named researchers are among its authors, but the record omits the first author and presents a partial group as the citation. The DOI and title match the relevant study; this is not evidence that the chapter relied on an unrelated result.

BV04 names Brus, Hartnett, Faulkner, Driesener "and colleagues". The publisher lists exactly those four authors. Use those four names without implying additional authors, and distinguish online publication on 7 June 2025 from the January 2026 issue.
