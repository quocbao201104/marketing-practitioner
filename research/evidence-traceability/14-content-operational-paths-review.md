## Suspected gaps not promoted to changes

### Adaptation does not always require a deeper transformation

Section 16 says not to merely shorten copy or swap surface conventions. In isolation that sentence could be read too broadly, but the same retrieved section opens with adapting only execution that matters and says to adapt when justified. The core explicitly preserves a direct path for sufficient narrow transformations. A legitimate shortening-only task is therefore already supported in context. No additional warning was added without a stronger conflicting case.

### A metric list is not a decision rule

Section 13 offers potential outcomes rather than a universal metric ranking. Its closing paragraph explicitly says selecting an outcome does not establish what a metric measures and points to content.measurement-evidence when needed. The core and Chapter 05 cover decision consequences, guardrails and uncertainty. Duplicating those rules here would not resolve a demonstrated new problem.

### Diagnosis ends with a check, but the task need not end there

Section 15's local purpose is to structure a platform diagnosis and identify the discriminating check. The core requires completing the requested job and returning from dependencies, and section 15 sends material causal work to Chapter 05. A separate ten-step intervention procedure would duplicate the existing decision owner. No evidence here warrants requiring an experiment when a direct observation resolves the task.

### Representation ownership and artifact completeness are already explicit

The operating guide prevents a generic message outline from replacing an environment-specific interaction job. The core requires enough domain-specific understanding and a usable next interaction in the final artifact. A factual inventory or caveat list is not accepted as completion merely because it includes the right state fields. No new output schema is needed.

### Distribution scope should not expand into a channel-portfolio system

These paths support choosing a participation form and adapting delivery in a scoped environment. They do not claim to solve a full media budget, channel portfolio or causal allocation problem. Such decisions require the relevant owners and evidence; this review does not expand the chapter's scope by treating every absent planning capability as a defect.
