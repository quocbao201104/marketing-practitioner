## Overall assessment

The chain contains useful boundaries: exploratory research need not manufacture a predetermined decision; sample recurrence is not population prevalence; target selection incorporates delivery constraints; customer desire does not establish product capability; and provisional wording is not customer evidence. The reviewed external material supports several narrower conceptual parents.

The principal issue is precision at a few transitions. A practical recommendation can inherit more scientific authority than its cited study establishes; a reassurance device can be mistaken for performance evidence; and distinct interview records can be described as independent evidence too readily. These warrant small qualifications, not a new framework or a controller redesign.

This review does not conclude that the skill produces effective marketing. It also does not require all useful practitioner synthesis to be experimentally validated before it can be used as explicitly provisional decision guidance.

## F1 — Separate claim evidence from commitments and risk reduction

Location: Chapter 03 §6 and Chapter 04 §4. Priority: first, because the ambiguity can affect factual promises.

Task: write a product page using a refund commitment, a plausible mechanism and no measured outcome data.

Current representation: guarantees, mechanisms, demonstrations and measured performance all appear in a proof menu. Chapter 04 also requires adequate evidence for wording, so this is an ambiguity despite an existing safeguard, not permission explicitly granted to fabricate results.

Gap: a commitment can support a statement about what the provider promises; it does not by itself demonstrate that the promised product outcome occurs. A mechanism can explain plausibility without establishing realized magnitude. Testability is a property of a claim, not evidence that it has passed a test.

Decision consequence: the agent may treat a refund commitment or a plausible mechanism as sufficient support for an outcome claim. For example, a conditional refund promise does not establish that every customer achieves the advertised time saving.

Smallest correction candidate: qualify the existing proof menu locally. Match evidence to the exact proposition; distinguish observed performance, capability demonstrations, commitments and risk reduction. Preserve terms/scope, and state that commitments, plausible mechanisms or falsifiability alone do not establish achieved effects. This is a logical clarification, not legal advice or a newly sourced empirical claim.

## F2 — Mark the bridge from language studies to writing heuristics

Location: Chapter 04 §6, with Chapter 03 §6 as a consistency check. Priority: second.

R07 studies customer–employee communication, with a perceived-listening explanation. Its Study 3 discussion does not identify fluency as the mediator of the concreteness effect. That supports keeping linguistic concreteness and processing fluency conceptually distinct. See the [article, Study 3 and discussion](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article/47/5/787/5873524).

R08 develops a measure of subjective processing ease, using several manipulations. It is relevant to measuring fluency, but does not establish that the handbook's particular combination of nouns, mechanisms and verbs is an optimal writing intervention. See the [accepted manuscript, measurement rationale and study overview](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/197646/7/197646.pdf).

Task: choose wording for a category-positioning headline with no campaign-specific outcome evidence.

Gap: the existing paragraph moves from scoped findings to generally useful writing priorities without explicitly labelling that move as practitioner synthesis. The next paragraph already rejects universal conversion superiority, which substantially limits the issue.

Decision consequence: a plausible editorial preference can be presented as a research-established performance recommendation, or unnecessary concreteness can displace an accurate category-level idea.

Smallest correction candidate: keep the narrow findings; label the writing priorities as practical heuristics selected for the reader's task. State that concreteness, processing ease and outcome effectiveness are distinct questions. No broad rewrite or new writing formula is warranted.

## F3 — Count distinct source units without assuming independence

Location: Chapter 01 §7. Priority: third.

Task: synthesize two interviews from the same buying committee whose participants discussed the topic beforehand.

Current representation: the example says that twelve notes from two interviews remain two independent sources. Subsequent text correctly handles shared wording origins, but that narrower safeguard does not resolve dependence between accounts more generally.

Gap: two source units are not automatically two independent confirmations. Shared events, organizations, incentives or prior discussion can create relevant dependence; independence also depends on the proposition being assessed.

Decision consequence: separate interview records can be overcounted as corroboration for a market need, buying criterion or independent convergence.

Smallest correction candidate: describe them as two interviews/root-source units; evaluate independence separately when it matters. Preserve the existing warning against multiplying evidence by processing it into more files. This is a logical qualification, not a proposed statistical independence test for every interview.

This is a static judgment about available instructions. It does not show that an agent follows them. F1–F3 concern ambiguity in that design, not measured failure rates.
