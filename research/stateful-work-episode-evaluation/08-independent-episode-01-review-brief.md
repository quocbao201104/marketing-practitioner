## 3. Specific attacks

### Construct validity

Does the required 10:30 reservation decision create a genuine later dependency, or can the task still be solved as a disguised single-shot answer?

Is the deposit/history rule material rather than decorative complexity?

### Visible sufficiency

Can an executor make a defensible reservation and final launch decision from visible evidence without hidden-world guessing?

Does the oracle rely on any unstated business fact?

### Pressure/control pair

Are `SWE-E01-P` and `SWE-E01-C` minimally different except for the coherent closure of the R2 audit result?

Can the changed R2 content actually justify the required semantic relation?

Could another collateral fact independently explain the expected difference?

### Oracle breadth

Can zero reservation, small reservation, larger bounded reservation, later reduction, or retained learning test all pass when defensible?

Does any predicate secretly encode a preferred allocation or Marketing Practitioner workflow?

### Temporal correctness

Can a private error repaired before consequence pass?

Can an authority violation remain failed after later budget recovery?

Can deposit state survive reservation reduction correctly?

### Event comparability

Is R2 genuinely exogenous and identically delivered within each no-skill/skill pair?

Does any hidden trigger remain trajectory-dependent?

### Control anti-skepticism

Is `E01-C01` strong enough to detect generic unchanged skepticism but broad enough to permit a defensible cautious decision?

### Pressure anti-overclaim

Does `E01-P01` require correction of the invalidated metric inference without forcing creator abandonment?

### Evaluator fixtures

Do the fixtures adequately attack:

- canonical bias;
- fluent wrong work;
- excessive caution;
- self-repair;
- historical violation;
- change/preserve sensitivity;
- missing telemetry;
- route-conformance leakage;
- event comparability;
- treatment contamination;
- burden reporting;
- mechanism-attribution laundering?

Are any fixtures themselves invalid, ambiguous, redundant in a materially harmful way, or capable of overfitting the oracle?

### Claim boundary

Does this design accidentally allow a live pilot to claim more than:

> a bounded condition difference / skill-availability effect under the exact frozen episode and execution regime, subject to treatment-integrity and comparative-validity gates?

## 6. Material finding standard

A finding is material only if it can plausibly cause:

- a wrong Episode 01 work verdict;
- a hidden-world oracle;
- pressure/control misclassification;
- a valid solution being rejected due to canonical-path bias;
- an invalid solution being accepted;
- historical consequence being erased or invented;
- invalid no-skill/skill comparison;
- treatment or mechanism attribution laundering;
- or inability of the fixture suite to detect a material evaluator defect before live execution.

Prefer the smallest bounded repair.
