# Use-case inventory and evidence

Date: 2026-09-10. Repository reviewed: `main@cee886f`.

This inventory records the work selected for the root [Use Cases](../USE-CASES.md) guide, its market evidence, current knowledge ownership, and limits. The expanded inventory was settled before revising the guide. It is documentation provenance, not runtime guidance or a new capability proposal.

## Selection method

Start with the recent research tracks and the jobs they investigated. Compare those jobs with public client or employer briefs, then check the current controller and relevant handbook sections. A candidate belongs in the guide when it has a recognizable trigger, a useful bounded result, and existing owners that can supply the required decisions.

The first six detailed episodes follow the recent performance-iteration, website-audit, lifecycle, interface-copy, cross-artifact-consistency, and search-intervention work. Four further episodes illustrate adjacent assignments found in public briefs. The initial ten were an editorial selection, not a completeness finding or capability ceiling.

The expanded pass also inspected public assignments for research, writing, social content, community participation, creator briefs, identity work, conversion experiments, and reporting. It retained 26 independently assignable tasks alongside the ten connected episodes. These are different views of work, not 36 disjoint capabilities: a focused task may be a useful component of a larger episode. Size is not an inclusion filter. A single caption or bounded competitor check belongs when it is a real requested result.

Separate entries need a materially different question, input contract, output, or decision boundary. Merely changing the platform, language, lifecycle stage, artifact count, or client industry does not earn another entry. A connected episode earns its place by showing consequential dependencies between outputs. Stop this pass when the inspected assignment families can be represented without hiding a distinct deliverable or inventing ownership; do not treat this selected sample as an exhaustive job-market inventory.

Public job descriptions establish that someone has requested a kind of work. They do not establish hiring outcomes, representative prevalence, sensible budgets, best practice, or this skill's effectiveness. The examples in the guide are original illustrative briefs; they are not quotations, client engagements, or benchmark results. Extra scenario details come from repository pressure cases or explicitly illustrative reasoning, not undocumented facts about the hiring clients.

## Scope choices

- Keep website audit and search diagnosis separate: a low-traffic trust page can serve its purpose, while a search symptom may require a technical check rather than content removal.
- Keep performance iteration and cross-artifact repair separate: one asks what evidence reopens; the other propagates an already-authoritative change.
- Keep lifecycle communication and interface copy separate: choosing whether/when to contact someone differs from expressing a supplied product state.
- Do not create separate cases for every channel, lifecycle stage, content format, or SEO/GEO/AEO label. Those can vary inside a job without creating a distinct useful result.
- Do not add a general marketing-automation, sales-operations, finance, technical-SEO, or UX-product-design capability merely because the sampled role also requests it.
- Include ordinary copy edits, captions, and standalone research. They are legitimate complete assignments; their small size is not a reason to hide them in a broad capability label.
- Keep focused work and connected episodes visible together. A copy rewrite, a decision about whether to change copy, and a cross-asset repair after a decision changes have different starting states and useful results.
- Consolidate variants within an assignment: do not create separate cases for five social platforms, every type of headline, or each lifecycle stage merely to increase the count.
- Defer a separate market-entry program, full SEO operation, autonomous media-buying service, or product-design service in this pass. Relevant bounded tasks may be covered, but the broader service claim requires its own concrete scope and evidence; omission is not a blanket incapability finding.
