# 16 — Founder-led Sales Decision Runtime

## 1. Scope: help a specific buying system reach or reject a justified commercial commitment

Use this chapter when the open question concerns **a specific buying situation** and the next decision depends on seller resource allocation, account/stakeholder selection, access, diagnosis, solution proof, buying-system risk/consensus, commercial commitment, or evidence-backed deal progression.

This chapter does not turn Marketing Practitioner into a generic sales playbook. It is a bounded decision runtime for the gap between resolved market/message/commercial strategy and a buyer-specific commitment.

Freeze the boundary:

```text
MARKETING / STRATEGY
market-level understanding
segment / ICP / JTBD
positioning
message
commercial system

!=

FOUNDER-LED SALES
specific Account × Buying Situation
specific decision work
specific stakeholders
specific evidence
specific commitments
specific pursuit allocation

!=

COPYWRITING
how the resolved communication should be expressed
```

Chapter 02 remains the owner of general market segmentation / ICP prioritization. Chapter 03 owns positioning and general value framing. Chapter 10 owns the default commercial system: package, pricing architecture, payment model, terms, allocation, and transition policy. Chapter 04 / `copywriting.*` owns expression after the seller has resolved what should be communicated. Chapter 05 owns formal causal diagnosis/experimentation when the question exceeds buyer-specific commercial diagnosis.

The central thesis is:

> Founder-led sales is an evidence-backed, nonlinear decision runtime for allocating scarce selling resources and helping a specific buying system move toward — or rationally away from — a justified commercial commitment.

The runtime is not a seller-stage funnel. Gartner describes complex B2B buying as nonlinear and involving revisited buying work rather than a predictable linear sequence [FS01]. SPIN is explicitly described as a logical framework rather than a rigid sequence [FS02]. MEDDPICC captures several important dimensions of complex opportunities but remains a qualification framework, not verified deal truth [FS03].

### 1.1 Activation boundary

Do not activate Founder-led Sales merely because the user mentions:

```text
lead
prospect
customer
email
demo
price
proposal
enterprise
objection
CRM
sales
```

Activate only when the unresolved decision is buyer-specific commercial work.

Examples:

```text
“Write this cold email from the approved message.”
→ Chapter 04 / copywriting.short-form
→ Chapter 16 only if who/why/ask/access strategy is still open
```

```text
“Which market segment should we target?”
→ Chapter 02
```

```text
“This specific account is interested. Should I spend founder time on a custom POC?”
→ Chapter 16
```

```text
“Our default annual plan should be $9k or $12k?”
→ Chapter 10
```

```text
“This buyer accepts the package but wants quarterly payment. Should we accept?”
→ Chapter 16 commercial-commitment work using Chapter 10’s resolved commercial system as input
```

### 1.2 Primary unit

Use:

```text
ACCOUNT × BUYING SITUATION
```

not the account generically.

One account can contain several independent buying situations with different problems, stakeholders, evidence, budgets, timing, or commercial consequences.

Keep:

```text
ACCOUNT FIT
!=
ACTIVE BUYING SITUATION
```

and:

```text
INTEREST
!=
OPPORTUNITY
```

A signal may justify a hypothesis. It does not become a buyer fact merely because it looks commercially promising.

## 2. Shared state, evidence discipline, and nonlinear routing

Use the smallest state representation that preserves decision-changing truth.

### 2.1 Evidence ledger

For material claims preserve, where relevant:

```text
CLAIM
What proposition is under consideration?

EPISTEMIC FORM
reported / observed / verified / inferred / hypothesis

SUPPORT STATUS
supported / contradicted / ambiguous / missing

PROVENANCE
Who or what supplied the evidence?

SCOPE
actor / team / function / account / buying situation / population

FRESHNESS
current / stale / superseded

EVIDENCE
What specifically supports the status?

OBSERVED_AT
When was the evidence or claim state established?
```

Never silently convert:

```text
buyer statement → verified fact
signal → buyer fact
friendly response → buying intent
estimate → measured value
old evidence → current evidence
```

`FORBIDDEN INFERENCE` is a reasoning constraint, not an evidence status.

### 2.2 Buyer-condition model

Represent only conditions that matter to the current buying situation.

```text
CONDITION
What must become sufficiently resolved?

CAPABILITY_OWNER
Which FS capability owns the decision?

BUYER_OWNER
Which buyer/external actor owns the next state change, if known and material?

STATE
UNKNOWN
NOT_STARTED
ACTIVE
RESOLVED
BLOCKED
REOPENED
NOT_REQUIRED

DEPENDENCIES
What must happen first?

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE
Why is this the current state?

NEXT CREDIBLE TRANSITION
What could materially change it?
```

Do not instantiate a universal mandatory buying checklist. A low-risk self-service purchase may need almost none of the state required by a complex enterprise commitment.

### 2.3 Commitment ledger

Use one commitment model across the runtime:

```text
ACTOR
ACTION / OBLIGATION
PURPOSE
AFFECTED CONDITION
DUE / TRIGGER
STATUS
FULFILLMENT EVIDENCE
```

Useful lifecycle:

```text
PROPOSED
→ ACCEPTED
→ OWNED
→ DUE
→ FULFILLED / MISSED / RENEGOTIATED / WITHDRAWN
```

Do not collapse:

```text
commitment
!= fulfillment

buyer effort
!= purchase intent

seller next step
!= buyer progress
```

### 2.4 Route by unresolved decision work

Do not route by topic noun:

```text
“security” → not automatically risk/consensus
“POC”      → not automatically proof
“price”    → not automatically negotiation
“legal”    → not automatically commercial work
“silence”  → not automatically access work
```

Ask instead:

> What material decision-relevant state is unresolved, and which owner can legitimately resolve it?

The runtime may move backward or sideways when evidence changes:

```text
proof → diagnosis
risk → proof
commercial terms → risk
commercial package → pursuit economics
progress review → stakeholder selection
```

That is model correction, not funnel regression.

## 3. FS1 — Pursuit allocation and resource routing

FS1 owns the global question:

> Given current evidence, should another increment of pursuit receive scarce capacity, how much, and through what mechanism?

Do not begin with fixed motion labels such as `enterprise`, `consultative`, `self-serve`, or `service-led`. Motion is an output of required work.

### 3.1 Forward-only pursuit economics

Every meaningful new commitment is a new investment decision.

Past effort does not justify future effort.

Consider forward commercial return, reusable learning, and credible strategic/option value against:

```text
founder attention
specialist time
engineering
customization
implementation
support
commercial/legal work
future obligations
opportunity cost
portfolio displacement
```

Do not invent a universal weighted score when the inputs are qualitative or uncertain.

Freeze:

```text
CAN SELL
!= SHOULD PURSUE
!= SHOULD USE HUMAN
!= SHOULD USE FOUNDER
!= HOW MUCH FOUNDER
```

### 3.2 Lowest-total-cost reliable mechanism

Choose the least costly mechanism that can **reliably** resolve the work, including buyer friction, delay, wrong-decision risk, and evidence quality.

Possible routes:

```text
SELF_SERVICE
STANDARD_HUMAN
SPECIALIST
FOUNDER
```

This is not “always automate.” Some consequential uncertainty genuinely requires contextual judgment or specialist/founder authority.

### 3.3 Founder-specific escalation

Founder involvement requires a reason beyond `important logo` or `large ACV`.

Possible reasons:

```text
cross-domain product/technical/business knowledge
seller-side uncertainty only founder can resolve
decision authority
high-novelty reusable learning
high-consequence exception
```

Founder involvement should normally be issue-scoped rather than account-scoped.

After the founder-specific job is resolved:

```text
DE-ESCALATE
```

Founding Sales supports founder involvement as a way to discover/refine and later transfer an initial motion, not as a permanent default [FS05].

### 3.4 Learning as a bounded subsidy

Learning can justify weak immediate economics only when the interaction can resolve a material uncertainty and the result is informative, transferable, decision-changing, and still marginally novel.

Reject:

```text
“we’re learning”
→ unlimited founder subsidy
```

Learning normally has diminishing marginal value. Repeated low-novelty founder work should eventually be productized, codified, delegated, intentionally priced as service, or stopped.

### 3.5 Global pursuit states

Only FS1 owns global pursuit allocation:

```text
ACTIVE
LIGHT_TOUCH
QUEUED
WAITING
TRIGGER_DEFERRED
STOPPED
```

Use `WAITING` when buyer/external work owns the known next transition. Do not manufacture seller activity.

Use `TRIGGER_DEFERRED` only when a concrete observable future condition can reactivate the pursuit.

Use `STOPPED` when no attractive next pursuit investment remains. The opportunity need not be proven impossible.

## 4. FS2 — Opportunity, account, and stakeholder selection

FS2 asks:

> What concrete buying situation is sufficiently evidence-backed to investigate, and which buying functions or people are plausible targets for discrimination?

FS2 does not own market segmentation or ICP design. Consume those from Chapter 02 when they are already resolved.

### 4.1 Signals create hypotheses, not buyer facts

Examples:

```text
funding
hiring
job change
technology adoption
pricing-page activity
OSS usage
referral
community participation
product usage
```

may change the value of investigation.

They do not by themselves establish:

```text
pain
budget
priority
purchase intent
willingness to change
```

### 4.2 Stakeholders by buying function

Map only what is decision-useful. Possible functions include:

```text
problem owner
beneficiary
economic authority
implementer
evaluator
risk owner
influencer
blocker
internal mobilizer
```

Do not infer function from title, seniority, friendliness, or one positive conversation.

MEDDPICC is useful evidence that complex sales can depend on economic authority, decision criteria/process, paper process, champion behavior, and competition [FS03]. Do not turn those nouns into mandatory fields or verified states.

### 4.3 Research stopping rule

Research to reduce decision-relevant uncertainty, not to maximize personalization.

Stop external account research when:

```text
additional research is unlikely to change selection/access judgment
or
buyer interaction becomes the cheaper reliable information source
```

FS2 can output a strong candidate while FS1 still leaves it queued because capacity is better used elsewhere.

## 5. FS3 — Engagement, access, and contact strategy

FS3 owns controlled access to the buyer’s decision system.

Ask:

> What decision-useful access is missing, and what is the smallest legitimate route/ask capable of obtaining it?

### 5.1 Access sufficiency

Possible access modes:

```text
direct
warm introduction
mediated
champion-mediated
async artifact
synchronous interaction
self-service
```

Choose from the current decision job, relationship, buyer burden, and available route — not a universal channel hierarchy.

FS3 does not own sentence-level outreach wording. Once `who / why / route / ask / timing` are resolved, Chapter 04 / `copywriting.short-form` may own expression.

### 5.2 Smallest sufficient ask

Prefer the smallest buyer ask that can produce material information, needed access, or real buyer-process movement.

Do not confuse:

```text
reply
meeting
click
engagement
```

with opportunity evidence.

### 5.3 Follow-up and multithreading are conditional transitions

Another contact attempt is justified only when it has a credible mechanism for improving access or resolving the decision.

Do not encode:

```text
always follow up N times
always multithread
always contact the most senior person
always get the decision maker
```

Stakeholder conflict and group decision problems can be material [FS07], but more contacts do not automatically improve a buying process.

### 5.4 Local stop

FS3 may conclude:

```text
NO_WORTHWHILE_ACCESS_TRANSITION
```

This is a local access result, not the global pursuit decision. Route the evidence to FS1.

## 6. FS4 — Discovery and diagnosis

FS4 reconstructs buyer reality without manufacturing pain.

Ask:

```text
What materially happens today?
What appears wrong or constrained?
What may cause it?
What rival explanations remain?
Why does it matter?
How is the current state handled?
What desired change is actually supported?
Why change — or why remain as-is?
```

### 6.1 Preserve epistemic distinctions

Keep separate:

```text
BUYER-STATED FACT
SELLER HYPOTHESIS
INFERRED DIAGNOSIS
DIRECT OBSERVATION
EXTERNALLY VERIFIED EVIDENCE
```

Do not lead the buyer into a problem because the product needs one.

### 6.2 Problem is not consequence

SPIN is useful because it distinguishes current situation/problem from implication and value of solving it [FS02]. Preserve the deeper principle without turning the acronym into a required script:

```text
PROBLEM EXISTS
!= PROBLEM MATTERS
```

A discovered friction may be real while change remains unjustified.

### 6.3 Causal and quantitative discipline

Do not teleport:

```text
workflow friction
→ exact revenue loss
```

Use exact quantification only when assumptions and evidence support it. Otherwise preserve magnitude, range, direction, uncertainty, or qualitative consequence.

When formal causal inference, experiment design, or treatment-effect interpretation becomes the open question, route to Chapter 05 rather than pretending buyer interviews establish causality.

### 6.4 Minimum sufficient diagnosis

Depth scales with:

```text
pursuit value
decision complexity
risk
cost of being wrong
buyer burden
seller effort
reversibility of the next step
```

FS1 governs whether deeper diagnosis is worth its resource cost.

Later proof, risk, or commercial evidence may reopen FS4 when it contradicts current reality, causal diagnosis, consequence, desired state, or change motive.

## 7. FS5 — Solution evaluation, value, and proof

FS5 is commercial proof design under uncertainty.

Ask:

> What exact proposition must be established for the buyer’s next decision, what evidence can validly establish it, and when should proof stop?

### 7.1 Separate proof levels

```text
PRODUCT UNDERSTANDING
What does it do?

SOLUTION CONFIDENCE
Do we have credible reason to believe it can solve this problem?

BUYER-SPECIFIC VALIDATION
Will it meet this buyer’s material requirement under relevant conditions?
```

Do not escalate automatically from explanation → demo → trial → POC → pilot.

### 7.2 Proof request is not proof need

Buyer asks:

```text
“Can we run a POC?”
```

First determine:

```text
what remains unknown
who owns the uncertainty
which decision it affects
what positive evidence changes
what negative evidence changes
```

Only then select the mechanism.

GitLab explicitly distinguishes trial access from a governed POV tied to customer outcome, scope, measurable criteria, participants, responsibilities, and a decision [FS06]. Use that as evidence for evaluation discipline, not as a universal workflow.

### 7.3 Evaluation contract

For material evaluation, resolve internally:

```text
DECISION
UNCERTAINTY
PROPOSITION
METHOD
SCOPE
BUYER INPUTS
SELLER INPUTS
SUCCESS / FAILURE CRITERIA
VALIDITY LIMITS
DECISION CONSEQUENCE
TERMINATION
```

Do not silently place custom development, migration, implementation, training, or consulting inside “validation.”

### 7.4 Value discipline

Keep separate:

```text
VALUE NARRATIVE
VALUE HYPOTHESIS
VALUE EVIDENCE
ATTRIBUTED VALUE
REALIZED VALUE
```

Technical success does not automatically establish business or economic success.

A business case synthesizes evidence; it cannot manufacture missing evidence.

### 7.5 Stop proving

Stop additional proof when, for example:

```text
no material evidentiary uncertainty remains
remaining uncertainty cannot change the next decision
uncertainty can be learned more cheaply after a bounded reversible commitment
pre-purchase proof ceiling is reached
a gating criterion validly rejects the path
remaining blocker is non-evidentiary
requested evidence is redundant
```

`STOP_PROVING` is local. It does not necessarily kill the deal.

## 8. FS6 — Risk, consensus, and decision enablement

FS6 asks:

> Why can a buying system that may already recognize value still not make a justified decision?

Do not use `objection` as the core reasoning object.

### 8.1 Barrier classes

Useful working classes:

```text
UNKNOWN
material decision information is missing

RISK
known downside remains unacceptable

CONSTRAINT
real policy/resource/requirement restricts action

CONFLICT
required actors hold incompatible assumptions,
criteria, interests, or risk tolerances

DEPENDENCY
one decision state requires another first

PRIORITY FAILURE
value may exist but change is not important enough now

GAP
product/offer genuinely fails a material requirement

REJECTION
buyer legitimately chooses not to proceed
```

A concern becomes a barrier only when it materially prevents justified commitment.

### 8.2 Status-quo resistance is not choice indecision

Keep separate:

```text
“Why should we change?”
```

and:

```text
“We want to change but are not confident choosing.”
```

JOLT research specifically identifies customer indecision as a different failure mode from indifference/status-quo resistance and warns that more FOMO can worsen some indecision [FS04].

Do not turn that distinction into a universal psychological diagnosis. Check evidence.

### 8.3 Consensus is not majority sentiment

All participants can individually say “yes” while holding incompatible implementation, value, timing, or risk assumptions.

Gartner reports material conflict in complex buying groups and associates consensus with deal quality in its surveyed population [FS07]. Do not generalize its prevalence to every segment or require a fixed committee topology.

### 8.4 FS6 → FS7 gate

Ask:

> If mutually acceptable commercial terms existed now, would any material non-commercial reason still prevent commitment?

If yes, remain in FS6 or route to the owner of the unresolved problem.

If no, commercial commitment work can become primary.

### 8.5 Local stop

FS6 may conclude:

```text
NO_CURRENT_VIABLE_DECISION_PATH
```

This is evidence for FS1. FS1 owns the global pursuit consequence.

## 9. FS7 — Commercial structure, negotiation, and commitment

FS7 controls the **specific commercial commitment** for this buying situation.

It does not redesign the company’s default pricing/package system unless Chapter 10 is reopened for a genuine design reason.

FS7 asks:

> Given the existing commercial system and this buyer-specific situation, what exact package of obligations is on the table, and is it mutually acceptable and executable?

### 9.1 Evaluate the whole package

Track material deltas in:

```text
scope
price
quantity
payment timing
term length
implementation responsibility
support
custom work
termination
risk allocation
procurement / execution requirements
```

Do not inspect concessions independently when their combination changes the economics or exposure of the package.

### 9.2 Negotiation is not discounting

Useful concepts include BATNA, reservation point, and ZOPA [FS09]. Apply them as context-dependent decision tools, not closing tricks.

Reject universal rules such as:

```text
always discount / never discount
always make the first offer
never do free pilots
always get something back
never accept buyer paper
longer term is always better
higher ARR is always a better deal
```

### 9.3 Commitment asymmetry

When seller exposure becomes large or hard to reverse while buyer commitment remains weak, trigger explicit review.

Example:

```text
seller:
6 weeks custom engineering

buyer:
“we’re very interested”
```

This is a warning condition, not an automatic rejection rule.

### 9.4 Package outcomes

Useful local outcomes:

```text
ACCEPT
COUNTER
RESIZE
DEFER
REOPEN
ESCALATE
WALK_CURRENT_PACKAGE
```

`WALK_CURRENT_PACKAGE` does not by itself equal global pursuit `STOPPED`.

If a buyer-requested package change materially alters founder/engineering burden or alternative use of capacity, route to FS1 before continuing negotiation.

## 10. FS8 — Deal progression, pipeline, forecast, and learning

FS8 is the evidence/history/control representation layer for live buying situations.

It does not own every specialist decision.

### 10.1 Seller activity is not buyer progress

Keep separate:

```text
EPISTEMIC PROGRESS
Do we understand reality better?

BUYER-PROCESS PROGRESS
Did a required buying condition materially advance?

DEAL VIABILITY
Did an acceptable purchase become more or less feasible?
```

A demo, proposal, follow-up, CRM update, or MAP does not establish buyer progress by itself.

Salesforce describes a MAP as a shared representation of what/when/who [FS08]. Treat the artifact as useful only when it reflects real buyer/seller commitments and dependencies.

### 10.2 Preserve state families separately

Do not collapse into one pipeline-stage field:

```text
buyer-condition state
evidence state
commitment state
pursuit allocation
forecast state
outcome state
learning state
```

A traditional CRM stage can remain a reporting projection.

### 10.3 Silence has weak semantics

```text
7 days silence
```

may mean internal work, vacation, politics, lost priority, budget freeze, competitor selection, or nothing requiring seller involvement.

Without stronger evidence:

```text
buyer state = UNKNOWN
```

Do not invent an objection or lost-interest diagnosis.

### 10.4 Forecast as a bounded claim

Preserve:

```text
WHAT
scope / amount

WHETHER
will the buyer choose and execute?

WHEN
inside which horizon?
```

Unknown timing remains unknown. Business targets must not contaminate the forecast.

### 10.5 Learning handoff

Sales observations are selected evidence, not market truth.

A useful learning packet can retain:

```text
observation
buyer/account context
decision consequence
evidence/source
frequency / denominator if known
alternative explanation
confidence
not established
```

Then route repeated patterns to the owner:

```text
market/entry pattern → Marketing / Chapter 02
buyer misunderstanding → Chapter 04
product gap → Product
commercial-system pattern → Chapter 10
proof pattern → FS5
consensus pattern → FS2 / FS6
pursuit economics → FS1
```

Do not turn one won/lost deal into strategy.

## 11. Owner boundaries and handoffs

Use these ownership rules before adding process.

### 11.1 FS1 is the single global pursuit-allocation owner

Local capabilities may terminate their own work:

```text
FS3 → NO_WORTHWHILE_ACCESS_TRANSITION
FS5 → STOP_PROVING
FS6 → NO_CURRENT_VIABLE_DECISION_PATH
FS7 → WALK_CURRENT_PACKAGE
```

Only FS1 sets global:

```text
ACTIVE
LIGHT_TOUCH
QUEUED
WAITING
TRIGGER_DEFERRED
STOPPED
```

### 11.2 FS8 represents pursuit state; it does not own pursuit economics

FS8 records state, history, freshness, commitments, forecast, outcome, and learning.

FS1 decides whether another increment of pursuit is worth scarce capacity.

### 11.3 FS3 owns access, not proof selection

```text
FS5
→ what evidence mechanism is required?

FS1
→ is it worth funding?

FS3
→ how do we obtain the required person/interface/access?
```

### 11.4 FS2 selection is not portfolio activation

FS2 can identify a strong candidate buying situation.

FS1 may still queue it because another use of constrained capacity is better now.

### 11.5 FS7 commercial economics is not FS1 portfolio economics

```text
FS7
Is the resulting agreement/package acceptable?

FS1
Is spending the next scarce unit trying to obtain agreement here still worthwhile?
```

### 11.6 Sales and customer research remain distinct

If the primary job is learning about a customer/world with no live commercial commitment decision, Chapter 01 owns the research.

Sales interaction may create useful research evidence, but Sales does not own market truth.

### 11.7 Sales and commercial design remain distinct

```text
Chapter 10
What commercial system should exist generally?

FS7
How should that system apply or be negotiated
with this specific buyer?
```

### 11.8 Sales and copywriting remain distinct

```text
Founder-led Sales
who / why / interaction / evidence / ask / commitment / next decision

Copywriting
how the resolved message should be expressed accurately and persuasively
```

### 11.9 Cross-owner handoff packet

A `founder-sales.handoffs` read must be sufficient to preserve decision-changing state across FS boundaries. Pass only what the next owner needs, but preserve at minimum when material:

```text
SUPPORTED
What is currently supported?

CONTRADICTED
What current claim or state is contradicted?

AMBIGUOUS / MISSING
What remains unresolved or unsupported?

BLOCKING CONDITION
What consequential state currently blocks a good decision?

WHY IT MATTERS
What decision consequence makes this blocker material?

NEXT DECISION
What decision now belongs to the receiving owner?

WHAT WOULD CHANGE THE DECISION
What observation, evidence, commitment, or state transition would alter it?

EXISTING COMMITMENTS
Who has committed to what, with current lifecycle status and due/trigger state?

RESOURCE / ACCESS REQUIREMENTS
What scarce resource or buyer/interface access is actually required?

CURRENT FS1 ALLOCATION
ACTIVE / LIGHT_TOUCH / QUEUED / WAITING / TRIGGER_DEFERRED / STOPPED,
when the allocation state is material to the receiving decision.

NEXT OWNER
Which FS or existing chapter owns the next decision job?

MUST-NOT-ASSUME
What tempting inference must not be promoted beyond current evidence?
```

For evidence carried across the handoff, retain the existing evidence-ledger dimensions rather than laundering a claim through prose:

```text
epistemic form
support status
provenance
scope
freshness
observed_at
supporting evidence
```

For commitments, retain actor, action/obligation, purpose, affected condition, due/trigger, lifecycle status, and fulfillment evidence. A local termination signal such as `STOP_PROVING`, `NO_WORTHWHILE_ACCESS_TRANSITION`, `NO_CURRENT_VIABLE_DECISION_PATH`, or `WALK_CURRENT_PACKAGE` remains local unless FS1 explicitly changes the global pursuit allocation.

A handoff may compress history, but it must not strengthen evidence, erase counterevidence, silently convert interest into commitment, replace a buyer-owned next transition with seller work, or lose the condition that would reopen/re-route the decision.

## 12. Compact runtime record

Do not create a large CRM form merely because fields are available.

For a material decision, the following record is usually enough:

```text
BUYING SITUATION
What concrete Account × Buying Situation is active?

CURRENT EVIDENCE
What is supported, contradicted, ambiguous, or missing?

BUYER CONDITIONS
What material state is unresolved or reopened?

PRIMARY DECISION JOB
Which FS owns it?

MINIMUM VALID INTERVENTION
What exactly could change the blocking state?

RESOURCE GATE
Is the intervention worth its full forward burden?

RESOURCE ROUTE
self-service / standard human / specialist / founder

ACCESS REQUIREMENT
What person/interface is needed, if any?

COMMITMENTS
Who has actually committed to what?

EXIT / RE-ROUTE CONDITION
What observation ends, changes, or reopens this work?

FS8 UPDATE
What actually changed after action or new evidence?
```

Do not ask the user to fill this record when the available context is already sufficient. It is an internal reasoning aid.

## 13. Anti-folklore and composition invariants

Preserve these unless stronger evidence for the current case justifies a narrower rule.

```text
FLS-I1
Seller activity is not buyer progress.

FLS-I2
A signal permits a hypothesis, not a buyer fact.

FLS-I3
Interest is not opportunity evidence.

FLS-I4
Problem is not priority or a sufficient change case.

FLS-I5
Product fit is not organizational ability to buy.

FLS-I6
Demo, trial, POC, POV, proposal, MAP, and CRM stage
are representations or mechanisms — not progress by label.

FLS-I7
Evidence retains provenance, scope, and freshness.

FLS-I8
Negative evidence may be healthy progress.

FLS-I9
Past pursuit effort never justifies future pursuit effort.

FLS-I10
Human/founder involvement is routed by unresolved work,
not ACV, company size, prestige, or stage.

FLS-I11
Use the lowest-total-cost reliable mechanism.

FLS-I12
More costly or irreversible commitments require stronger support.

FLS-I13
De-escalate human/founder intensity after its job is resolved.

FLS-I14
Learning may subsidize weak immediate economics only when
it resolves material reusable uncertainty.

FLS-I15
Founder repetition does not prove repeatability.

FLS-I16
Friendly contact does not establish internal mobilization.

FLS-I17
Majority-positive stakeholders do not establish consensus.

FLS-I18
A known product gap must not be reframed as an objection
or evidence problem.

FLS-I19
Adequate proof does not imply commitment.

FLS-I20
When the blocker is non-evidentiary, stop proving and route.

FLS-I21
Commercial negotiation must not solve unresolved diagnosis,
proof, value, or consensus work through price/terms.

FLS-I22
A winnable deal may rationally deserve global STOPPED.

FLS-I23
Buyer-owned work may justify doing nothing now.

FLS-I24
A stopped/lost deal may still produce bounded learning.

FLS-I25
Sales evidence is strategy input, not strategy truth.
```

The runtime must be able to represent all of these without contradiction:

```text
tiny deal → founder-heavy
when bounded reusable learning is material

huge deal → self-service
when no consequential human work exists

winnable deal → stop
when forward economics are poor

quiet deal → wait
when buyer/external work owns the next transition

founder → specialist → self-service
inside one account as uncertainty resolves

good opportunity → queued
when capacity is better used elsewhere

repeatable normal deal → no founder
although the founder could personally sell it better
```

If the runtime cannot produce those outcomes, it has likely regressed toward stage-, activity-, ACV-, or founder-centric folklore.
