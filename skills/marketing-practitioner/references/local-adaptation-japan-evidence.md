# Local Adaptation Evidence — Japanese Relationship Realization

Scope: evidence for `JP-LANG-HON-01` and `JP-LANG-PERM-01`.

This ledger is deliberately narrow. It does **not** establish a Japan market profile, a universal Japanese politeness preference, a customer-service script, a regional-form lookup table, or a marketing-effect claim. Evidence here is used only to support bounded Japanese target-language realization constraints and their non-transfer boundaries.

Keep:

```text
LINGUISTIC FUNCTION
!= POPULATION ACCEPTABILITY

POPULATION ACCEPTABILITY
!= MARKETING EFFECT

OFFICIAL GUIDANCE
!= UNIVERSAL RULE FOR EVERY JAPANESE VARIETY / COMMUNITY

LANGUAGE CHANGE
!= PROOF THAT BASE SEMANTICS DISAPPEARED EVERYWHERE
```

## [JPLA01] Agency for Cultural Affairs — Keigo basics; Kenjōgo I vs Kenjōgo II

**Source:** 文化庁, 第二話「敬語の基本」理解度チェックの解答.

**URL:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/kokugo_nihongo/kokugo_shisaku/keigo/chapter2/detail.html

**Type:** Japanese government language guidance based on the `敬語の指針` framework.

**Supports:**

- `謙譲語Ⅰ` can honor the person who is the `向かう先` of an action.
- `謙譲語Ⅱ` can function as deferential expression toward the current addressee.
- When action target and addressee differ, the two forms can have materially different honorific orientation.
- `伺う` and `参る` are not interchangeable merely because both can be described broadly as humble/deferential forms.

**Does not establish:**

- a deterministic rule for who always deserves honorification;
- a global formal/casual score;
- universal applicability to every regional Japanese honorific system;
- any marketing-effect claim.

## [JPLA02] Agency for Cultural Affairs — `伺います` vs `参ります` with a third-party teacher

**Source:** 文化庁, 第六話「間違いやすい敬語（3）～謙譲語I VS 謙譲語II」理解度チェックの解答.

**URL:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/kokugo_nihongo/kokugo_shisaku/keigo/chapter6/detail.html

**Type:** official applied honorific guidance.

**Supports:**

- a concrete case where the speaker addresses teacher A while talking about visiting teacher B;
- `参る` is deferential toward the addressee but does not honor the third-party visit target;
- `伺う` can honor the visit target.

**Does not establish:**

- that `伺う` should always replace `参る`;
- that third-party people should always be honorified;
- one fixed hierarchy based on profession or age.

## [JPLA03] Agency for Cultural Affairs — customer action and wrong honorific target

**Source:** 文化庁, 第四話「間違いやすい敬語（1）～尊敬語 VS 謙譲語I」理解度チェックの解答.

**URL:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/kokugo_nihongo/kokugo_shisaku/keigo/chapter4/detail.html

**Type:** official applied honorific guidance.

**Supports:**

- `担当者に伺ってください` is inappropriate when a receptionist tells a customer to ask the staff member;
- using `伺う` there honorifically orients toward the action target (staff) rather than correctly realizing the customer's action;
- globally respectful intent can therefore still produce a wrong Japanese honorific target.

**Does not establish:**

- that all uses of `伺う + ください` are impossible in every context;
- a customer-role lookup table;
- population dislike or preference claims.

## [JPLA04] Agency for Cultural Affairs — uchi/soto and organizational-context exceptions

**Source:** 文化庁, 第七話「場面で異なる敬語～ウチとソト～」理解度チェックの解答.

**URL:** https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/kokugo_nihongo/kokugo_shisaku/keigo/chapter7/detail.html

**Type:** official applied sociolinguistic / honorific guidance.

**Supports:**

- `uchi / soto` can materially affect how own-side organizational people are referred to when speaking externally;
- organizational side is a real contextual factor in Japanese realization;
- the school/parent example provides a counterexample to mechanical application: a colleague teacher may still be referred to as `田中先生` because another interactional frame is salient;
- job title can provide a more neutral representation in some contexts.

**Does not establish:**

- `own organization → always de-honorify`;
- `external person → always honorify`;
- a universal precedence rule where role always beats uchi/soto or vice versa;
- a need for an executable organization relationship graph.

## [JPLA05] Agency for Cultural Affairs — `〜させていただく` baseline conditions and contextual suitability

**Sources:**

- 文化庁, 第三話「敬語のTPO～依頼の仕方～」理解度チェックの解答: https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/kokugo_nihongo/kokugo_shisaku/keigo/chapter3/detail.html
- 文化庁, 平成19年度「国語に関する世論調査」の結果について: https://www.bunka.go.jp/tokei_hakusho_shuppan/tokeichosa/kokugo_yoronchosa/h19/

**Type:** official guidance plus Japanese public-language survey interpretation.

**Supports:**

- baseline analysis of `〜させていただく` as an own-side action associated with permission from addressee/third party plus benefit to the speaker;
- perceived suitability varies with how strongly those conditions are satisfied or can plausibly be construed;
- closure/meeting-ending cases can be better realized with `〜いたします` when permission/benefit framing is not supported;
- genuine permission-request cases fit the baseline mechanism more directly.

**Does not establish:**

- `no explicit permission → construction always forbidden`;
- `construction present → factual permission definitely existed`;
- current population-wide preference from historical survey data;
- legal or business authorization from wording alone.

## [JPLA06] National Institute for Japanese Language and Linguistics — historical/corpus change in `させていただく`

**Source:** 国立国語研究所 ことば研究館, 「確認させていただいてもよろしいですか？」なんて言われると目が点になります.

**URL:** https://kotoba.ninjal.ac.jp/qa/yokuaru/qa-71/

**Type:** NINJAL public explanation grounded in corpus research and historical pragmatics.

**Supports:**

- historical growth of `サセテイタダク` relative to related benefactive constructions;
- contemporary examples exist where the other party is weakly or not materially involved;
- the construction can contribute to interpersonal distance/deference;
- contemporary usage cannot be interpreted only through a literal permission transaction.

**Does not establish:**

- every contemporary use has lost permission/benefit meaning;
- the form is universally preferred or disliked;
- age/region lookup rules.

## [JPLA07] Shiina 2024 — grammaticalization and newer deferential uses of `させていただく`

**Source:** 椎名 美智, 「シン・させていただく」の誕生秘話 — 文法化と敬意漸減の影響, *待遇コミュニケーション研究* 21 (2024), 50–65.

**URL:** https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tcg/21/0/21_50/_article/-char/ja

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.32252/tcg.21.0_50

**Type:** Japanese historical-pragmatics / honorific research.

**Supports:**

- analysis of pragmatic expansion from benefactive/humble use toward newer deferential and, in some analyses, beautifying uses;
- original permission/benefit meanings can weaken in some contemporary examples;
- `させていただく` has medium-volatility pragmatic semantics rather than one timeless binary rule.

**Does not establish:**

- permission/benefit is absent from all current use;
- all newer uses are acceptable in every context;
- a prescriptive ban or automatic replacement rule.

## [JPLA08] NHK Broadcasting Culture Research Institute — 2023 Japanese language variation survey

**Source:** 塩田 雄大, とりあえず“そのうち食事でもしましょう“ — 2023年「日本語のゆれに関する調査」から(2), *放送研究と調査* 74(2), 2024, 34–59.

**URL:** https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bunken/74/2/74_34/_article/-char/ja

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.24634/bunken.74.2_34

**Type:** NHK language-variation survey report.

**Supports:**

- acceptability/preference for surveyed `させていただく` expressions varies by respondent attributes and context;
- age and regional differences appear in the tested material;
- population variation is strong enough to reject a universal “Japanese people prefer/dislike this construction” rule.

**Does not establish:**

- causal marketing effects;
- a deterministic age/region choice rule;
- that every construction/context shares the same distribution.

## [JPLA09] NHK Broadcasting Culture Research Institute — 2025 Japanese language variation survey, published 2026

**Source:** 塩田 雄大, “一生スマホをいじっている” 20代では7割近くが使う — 2025年「日本語のゆれに関する調査」から（1）, *放送研究と調査* 76(1-2), 2026, 30–59.

**URL:** https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bunken/76/1-2/76_30/_article/-char/ja/

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.24634/bunken.76.1-2_30

**Type:** recent NHK language-variation survey report.

**Supports:**

- for the surveyed `ネギを入れさせていただいてもよろしいですか` comparison, support for the `させていただく` form varied systematically by age;
- current variation does not fit a simple “young speakers invented/prefer the form” story;
- newer evidence should constrain time-sensitive population-acceptability statements.

**Does not establish:**

- a general rule that older speakers always prefer `させていただく`;
- construction-level semantics by itself;
- marketing outcome claims.

## [JPLA10] Immigration Services Agency / Agency for Cultural Affairs — Easy Japanese guidance

**Sources:**

- 出入国在留管理庁, やさしい日本語ガイドライン: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/support/portal/plainjapanese_guideline.html
- supporting official material: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/930005857.pdf

**Type:** Japanese government accessibility / communication guidance.

**Supports:**

- for Easy Japanese aimed at comprehensibility, avoid `尊敬語 / 謙譲語` in principle while retaining basic polite `丁寧語` / `です・ます` style;
- accessibility can legitimately reduce honorific complexity without implying disrespect;
- a complex deferential expression can be simplified when this communication regime actually applies.

**Does not establish:**

- Easy Japanese should be used for all foreign residents or all Japanese-language marketing;
- Japanese communication is generally better without honorifics;
- a country-triggered accessibility rule.

## [JPLA11] Agency for Cultural Affairs — regional honorific diversity

**Sources:**

- 文化庁, 敬語の指針 / regional honorific diversity material: https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/bunkashingikai/kokugo/hokoku/pdf/keigo_tosin.pdf
- supporting regional material: https://www.bunka.go.jp/seisaku/bunkashingikai/kokugo/kokugo/kokugo_33/pdf/siryou_2.pdf

**Type:** official Japanese language-policy / honorific guidance.

**Supports:**

- Japanese regional varieties contain honorific systems that can differ from common/standard Japanese in form and use;
- Kansai `〜はる` is one official example, including distributions with own-side persons that differ from common standard expectations;
- a Japanese-language unit must not claim universal authority over stronger scoped regional/community evidence.

**Does not establish:**

- infer `〜はる` from Kansai/Osaka geography alone;
- all Kansai varieties use the form identically;
- a regional dialect resolver.

## Evidence synthesis for `JP-LANG-HON-01`

The strongest supported delta beyond generic localization guidance is:

```text
JAPANESE HONORIFIC REALIZATION
!= ONE GLOBAL FORMALITY SCALAR

ADDRESSEE
!= ACTOR / REFERENT
!= ACTION TARGET

GLOBAL "BE RESPECTFUL"
!= CORRECT HONORIFIC ORIENTATION

UCHI / SOTO
= CONTEXTUAL FACTOR
!= DETERMINISTIC RULE

STANDARD JAPANESE
!= UNIVERSAL REGIONAL AUTHORITY
```

The sources justify a bounded honorific-target realization constraint. They do not justify inferring hierarchy, organization side, customer deference level, or regional forms from broad labels.

## Evidence synthesis for `JP-LANG-PERM-01`

The strongest supported delta is:

```text
INTRODUCING させていただく
!= SEMANTICALLY NEUTRAL POLITENESS TRANSFORM

BASELINE ANALYSIS
→ PERMISSION + BENEFIT SENSITIVE

CONTEMPORARY LANGUAGE CHANGE
→ REVERSE INFERENCE IS UNSAFE

EXISTING させていただく
!= VERIFIED FACTUAL PERMISSION / BENEFIT

POPULATION VARIATION
!= FORM LOOKUP KEY
```

These sources justify preserving already-resolved permission, authority, agency, obligation, benefit, and responsibility state through Japanese realization. They do **not** authorize inferring those states from wording alone or banning the construction whenever explicit permission evidence is absent.
