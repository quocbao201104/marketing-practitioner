# TikTok Shop Vietnam Applicability Evidence

Vietnam-specific first-party evidence for deciding whether an existing TikTok Shop mechanism documented primarily from US Seller Academy can be applied locally. These records supplement, rather than replace, `tiktok-shop-evidence.md`.

## [TV01] TikTok Shop Vietnam — Product Traffic analytics

TikTok Shop Seller Center Vietnam. **Product Traffic.** Published 2026-06-17. Applies to Vietnam.

Direct source:

`https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8623594494625537&lang=en`

Use: current Vietnam evidence for product-level traffic and conversion analysis, including source/context breakdowns across Seller LIVE, Video, Product Card, Affiliate, and Shop Tab, with report-specific metric definitions and aggregation caveats.

Boundary:

- report attribution/source labels do not establish causal incrementality;
- similar metric names across reports do not guarantee identical units, denominators, timing, or aggregation;
- the current documentation includes definition/label details that should be preserved rather than silently normalized by the practitioner.

## [TV03] TikTok Shop Vietnam — Link Products tool

TikTok Shop Seller Center Vietnam. **Link Products tool.** Published 2025-10-29. Applies to Vietnam.

Direct source:

`https://seller-vn.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=496374274639617&lang=en`

Use: current Vietnam evidence that eligible short videos posted within the documented window can receive a first product link after posting, and that the creator UI can recommend products for the video. This supports a creator-side product-recommendation mechanism distinct from shopper-side For You or Shop ranking.

Boundary:

- the documented Vietnam flow concerns adding a product to eligible currently unlinked videos;
- it does not establish general editing/replacement of an existing product link;
- it does not establish universal availability of the dedicated US unavailable-target Product Relinking flow documented in TTS08;
- system product suggestions do not guarantee factual video-product alignment and do not disclose shopper-side ranking signals.
